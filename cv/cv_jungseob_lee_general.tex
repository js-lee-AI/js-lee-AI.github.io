\eentryhead{KURE-v3, Korean-English Retrieval Embedding Model}{2026}{Core Contributor, Korea University NLP \& AI Lab and HIAI Institute}{Data synthesis, training optimization, experiments}
\begin{points}
  \item The newest dense model of the \textbf{KURE} family of Korean-English retrieval models (250+ GitHub stars), released on Oct~20, 2026 with open weights on Hugging Face and a public API.
  \item Synthesized the training data, 3M+ query-document triplets with mined hard negatives and false-negative filtering.
  \item Optimized training with teacher-score distillation (a min-max normalized KL loss), Matryoshka embeddings from 2,048 down to 256 dimensions, and inputs of up to 8,192 tokens.
  \item Ran the experiments on the nine MTEB(kor, v2) retrieval tasks and English RTEB against 20+ open retrievers.
  \item With 1.1B parameters KURE-v3 scores 0.803 average nDCG@10, above much larger dense retrievers such as Qwen3-Embedding-8B and the 27B harrier-oss-v1, and keeps 99\% of its score at 1,024 dimensions.
  \item \lnk{Model}{https://huggingface.co/nlpai-lab/KURE-v3}\enspace\lnk{GitHub}{https://github.com/nlpai-lab/KURE}\enspace\lnk{API}{https://kure.kullm.ai/}
\end{points}
