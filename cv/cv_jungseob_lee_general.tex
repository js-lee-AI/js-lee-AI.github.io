\sect{Summary}
Integrated M.S./Ph.D. researcher at the Korea University NLP Lab (Advisor: Prof.~Heuiseok Lim), specializing in large language models and natural language processing, with a focus on agentic LLMs, LLM efficiency, and efficient reasoning. First-author research has been published at \textbf{EMNLP 2026} (Main), \textbf{ACL 2025} (Oral), and \textbf{Findings of ACL 2024}. Four papers were accepted at \textbf{EMNLP 2026} (2 Main, 2 Findings), with co-authored work at \textbf{ICLR 2026} and a series of preprints under review. Recipient of the \textbf{Best Paper Award (Top~1)} at HCLT 2025. Served as the post-training Project Manager and Korea University representative for \textbf{VAETKI}, Korea's national Sovereign AI Foundation Model. Author of 30+ peer-reviewed papers across top-tier NLP venues and journals.\par\vspace{4pt}
{\raggedright\textbf{Research Interests:} Large Language Models, Natural Language Processing, Agentic LLM, LLM Efficiency, Efficient~Reasoning\par}
